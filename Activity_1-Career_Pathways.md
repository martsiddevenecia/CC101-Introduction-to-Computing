## III. Background

Computing is not just "programming." The field branches into several distinct career pathways: Software Engineering, Systems & Cloud Infrastructure, Data Science & AI Engineering, Cybersecurity & Threat Defense, and Information Systems & Enterprise IT. Each pathway has its own career roadmap — entry-level, mid-level, senior-level, and expert/leadership roles.
